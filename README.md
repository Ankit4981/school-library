## 🏛️ Brand & Design Philosophy

- **Official Crest & Colors**: Designed around the royal navy blue (`#0f2b5c`, `#1e3a8a`), gold/orange CTA accents (`#f59e0b`, `#d97706`), crisp white/light blue surfaces (`#ffffff`, `#f0f4fc`), with green reserved solely for success status and red for alerts.
- **Realistic Library Experience**: Realistic digital book cards with 3D spine and page trims, category badges, grade levels, reading time, audio flags, bookmarking, and "Read Now" launchers.
- **100% Free School Access**: An educational hub for students with no fees, no selling, and full borrowing privileges approved by the school librarian.
- **Strict Separation of Concerns**: Student browsing is clean and clutter-free without administrative clutter. All librarian controls (catalog management, user approvals, quiz question authoring, news publishing) reside in a dedicated **Librarian Management Hub**.
- **No Exposed Credentials**: Clean authentication without exposing administrator or default passwords in the UI.

## 📚 Library Sections & Features

1. **Home Discovery Hub**:
   - Academic hero banner with motto: *"Discover. Read. Learn. Participate. — 100% Free Access for Every Student with School Permission."*
   - Featured Digital Books carousel.
   - Saved Bookmarks / Continue Reading quick shelf.
   - Explore Library Wings (Junior, Secondary, Biographies).
   - Popular This Week & Recently Added shelves.
   - Interactive teasers for *Test Your Wits*, *Suggest a Book*, *Vote for the Next Book*, *Avid Reader Spotlight*, and *Daily Newspapers & Campus News*.

2. **🧸 Junior Library Wing**:
   - Nursery Rhymes, Short Stories, Flip Books, Bedtime Audio.
   - Age/Grade filters (Pre-K to Grade 5), real-time search.

3. **📚 Secondary Library Wing**:
   - Academics & Science, Graphic Comics, World Literature Novels, Visual Encyclopedias, Audio Books.
   - Middle School (Grades 6–8), Secondary (Grades 9–10), Senior Secondary (Grades 11–12) filters.

4. **👑 Biographies & Great Minds**:
   - Inspiring life stories of Scientists, Inventors, Leaders, Indian Personalities, and Global Figures.

5. **📖 In-Site Digital Reading Suite**:
   - 100% in-portal reader modal with zero external redirects.
   - Embeds Google Drive collections (`embeddedfolderview` with grid & list toggles) and documents (`/preview`).
   - Supports self-hosted PDFs, interactive HTML novels, and audiobooks.
   - Fullscreen / Theater Mode (`⛶`) and live session reading timers.

6. **🧠 Test Your Wits (Interactive Quiz Arena)**:
   - Junior Trivia, Secondary Academic Wits, Great Minds Trivia.
   - Live progress indicator, instant explanation boxes, final score accuracy rating, reading points, and full answer review mode.

7. **💡 Suggest a Book**:
   - Student recommendation desk with tracking for personal past requests.
   - Librarian demand breakdown.

8. **🗳️ Vote for the Next Book**:
   - Democratic polling booth for student-driven library acquisitions with live percentage progress bars.

9. **🏆 Avid Readers of the Month**:
   - Gold-podium recognition for School Campus Star Readers and Digital Portal Star Readers with personal reading reflections.

10. **📰 Daily Newspapers & Campus News**:
    - Digital Newspaper Stand with editions of *The Hindu (In School)*, *Indian Express Explorer*, *Times NIE*, and *Viraj Campus Chronicle*.
    - Filterable announcements and circulars for Today, This Week, and Archive.

11. **👤 Student Reading Passport & Free Digital Borrowing**:
    - Progression ranks (*Bronze Scholar 🥉*, *Book Explorer 🥈*, *Master Scholar 🥇*, *Grand Bibliophile 💎*).
    - Active Borrowing & Digital Loans shelf with 14-day duration countdowns and instant return controls.
    - Breakdown of books read, audio consumed, quizzes solved, and chronological activity log.

12. **🛡️ Librarian & Admin Management Hub**:
    - Real-time catalog analytics and user verification queue.
    - **📦 Smart ZIP Archive Auto-Importer**: Upload a `.zip` archive of PDFs; the system auto-extracts, cleans titles, reads page counts, and auto-sorts by genre.
    - **⚡ Batch Link Importer & PDF Link Extractor**: Parses hyperlinked catalog PDFs or paste lists.
    - Quiz bank editor & campus news publisher.

## 🚀 Running the Application

1. Open a terminal in the project directory:

   ```powershell
   npm install
   npm start
   ```

2. Open your browser and navigate to [`http://localhost:3000`](http://localhost:3000).
