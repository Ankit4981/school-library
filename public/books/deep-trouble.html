Book Overview:
Billy Deep and his sister Sheena are visiting their marine biologist uncle, Dr. D, on his Caribbean research boat Cassandra. While exploring the coral reefs, they encounter real hammerhead sharks, a mysterious glowing mermaid, and a twelve-tentacled sea monster. But when greedy smugglers kidnap the mermaid, Billy and his family must pull off a daring ocean rescue.
