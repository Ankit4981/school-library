Viraj Science Olympiad Team Triumphs at State Level Championship

By Senior Editorial Board • Special Edition

Students of Viraj International School delivered a stellar performance at the Maharashtra State Science & Innovation Olympiad held this past weekend. Led by Grade 10 student researchers, the team presented an innovative solar-powered smart irrigation sensor model designed specifically for local farmers in the Palghar and Boisar agricultural belts.

The judging committee, comprised of senior academicians and scientists, lauded the project for its direct societal utility, low manufacturing cost, and environmental sustainability. "Our goal was not just to win an award, but to build technology that helps our community conserve water," shared team lead Aarav Sharma.

Principal Dr. Manasvi congratulated all participants during morning assembly, encouraging every student to utilize the Digital Library Hub's new STEM and science encyclopedia archives to prepare for future national-level hackathons.
