Clean Ocean Robotics: How High Schoolers Are Removing Microplastics

An inspiring youth collective has engineered autonomous solar skimming drones designed to collect floating microplastics along coastlines. Tested across beaches in western India, these miniature rovers can filter out particles smaller than 5 millimeters using biodegradable nano-mesh membranes.

Marine biologists note that such grassroots student inventions demonstrate the enormous power of applied STEM education in addressing real-world environmental crises.
