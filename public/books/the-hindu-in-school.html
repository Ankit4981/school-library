Editorial: The Power of Cultivating a Daily Reading Habit

In a world increasingly dominated by bite-sized algorithmic clips and fleeting notifications, sustained long-form reading stands out as a superpower. Studies show that students who spend 20 minutes each day reading non-fiction and classic literature develop higher cognitive retention, superior vocabulary, and deeper empathy.

Libraries—whether physical shelves or well-curated digital repositories like Viraj International School's Knowledge Desk—serve as sanctuaries for deep work. Let this week be an invitation to put away digital distractions and immerse in a chapter of history, science, or adventure.
