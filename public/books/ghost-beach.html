Book Overview:
Jerry and Terri Sadler spend the summer in New England with their elderly cousins Brad and Agatha. Exploring a cliffside beach, they discover an ancient cemetery with gravestones from 1642 bearing the names of their new playmates. When a mysterious hermit in a candlelit cave claims the children are ghosts from the Great Winter, a horrifying plot twist awaits them.

“I can’t!” “Yes, you can! You’ve got to keep trying!” “Ohhh!” I let out a low cry as two hands grabbed my ankles. Now I was caught. We were both trapped.
