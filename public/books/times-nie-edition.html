India's Next-Gen Space Telescopes to Map Distant Galaxies

Astrophysicists at ISRO and premier national laboratories have finalized designs for a next-generation orbital astronomical observatory. The mission will explore exoplanet atmospheres, cosmic radiation bursts, and dark matter signatures across deep space.

High school astronomy clubs across India have been invited to participate in the data-tagging initiative, allowing student scholars to examine real optical imagery sent from orbital observation satellites.

🧬 Science & Curiosity

Why Do Leaves Change Color? As autumn approaches, trees decrease chlorophyll production. Hidden pigments like carotenoids and anthocyanins emerge, turning foliage into shades of gold and amber.
